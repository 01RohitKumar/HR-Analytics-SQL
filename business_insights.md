# HR Analytics — Business Insights

## 1. Department-wise Attrition
- Sales has the highest attrition rate at 20.63%.
- Human Resources has an attrition rate of 19.05%.
- Research & Development has the lowest attrition rate at 13.84%.

## 2. Job Role-wise Attrition
- Sales Representatives have the highest attrition rate at 39.76%.
- Laboratory Technicians have an attrition rate of 23.94%.

## 3. Overtime Analysis
- 416 employees work overtime.
- The average monthly income of overtime employees is 6,548.55.
- The average monthly income of non-overtime employees is 6,484.93.

## 4. Age Group-wise Attrition
- Employees under 25 have the highest attrition rate at 39.18%.
- The 25–34 age group has an attrition rate of 20.22%.

## Conclusion
The analysis shows higher attrition in the Sales department, among Sales Representatives, and in the under-25 age group. These findings can help HR teams identify areas for further investigation.
